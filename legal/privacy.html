Privacy policy

KORD (Personal Operating Model) for iPhone · Last updated October 26, 2026

In short: KORD works on your iPhone. Your health data stays on your device. There is no KORD account and no KORD server that receives your data, and KORD has no ads, no tracking and no data selling.

What KORD reads

  • Apple Health data you allow, such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, distance, workouts, energy and VO₂ max. This includes data that apps like Apple Watch, Garmin Connect, Oura or WHOOP write to Apple Health. KORD only reads; it does not write to Apple Health.
  • Lab results you explicitly choose to share from Apple Health’s health records, to show your blood-test history.
  • Motion activity from your iPhone, only if you turn it on, to spot possible runs and rides recorded without GPS.
  • What you enter: your name, birth month and year, the sex used for health benchmarks, goals, training preferences, safety information, check-ins and notes.

How it is used

To work out your Capacity, your three daily actions, your training plan and your weekly review. All of this is calculated on your iPhone. Apple Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with or sold to anyone.

Where it is stored

  • In KORD’s private storage on your iPhone.
  • Copies of Apple Health data, blood findings, doctor notes, clinical follow-ups and training-pause records are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Restoring or moving to a new phone does not bring them back. Apple Health keeps its own data under its own protections.
  • Your settings, training plan and plan decisions are included in your device backups, which Apple encrypts.
  • You can turn on App Lock so Face ID is needed to open KORD.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing personal, unless you choose to share it.

  • Content downloads. KORD downloads signed lists of reviewed exercise videos and feature settings from GitHub Pages. These requests contain no personal information, though, like any web request, they reveal your IP address to GitHub.
  • Race search. If you search for a race, the name you type is sent to RunSignup to find matching events.
  • Links you open, such as YouTube videos or health information sites, are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.
  • Sharing. Exports, such as a doctor-visit summary or a tester decision log, leave your phone only when you tap Share and choose where to send them.

Your choices

  • Change what KORD can read at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.
  • Delete everything KORD has stored with Settings › Erase all data in the app, or by deleting the app.

Children

KORD is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the app asks you to review the new version.
